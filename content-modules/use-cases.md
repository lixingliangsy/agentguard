# AgentAegis — Use Cases

### Tool-using agents
- **Pain:** Email/SQL/HTTP tools can take irreversible actions.
- **How AgentAegis helps:** Rate each tool, propose I/O filters, suggest blocklists.
### EU-bound agents
- **Pain:** Need high-level EU AI Act mapping for oversight.
- **How AgentAegis helps:** Map highest-risk actions to relevant obligations (decision-support).
### Risk appetite tuning
- **Pain:** Strict vs permissive policies differ by product.
- **How AgentAegis helps:** Respect stated appetite in recommendations.
### Platform security
- **Pain:** Need explainable guardrail reports for reviews.
- **How AgentAegis helps:** Deterministic tool-risk checklist with rulesetVersion.

ref: OWASP Top 10 for AI Agents · EU AI Act (Reg. 2024/1689)
